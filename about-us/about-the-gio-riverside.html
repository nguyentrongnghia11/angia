About

Situated in the Eastern part of the city, The Gió Riverside enjoys a prime location at the confluence of the Ngoc River and the Dong Nai River. Positioned as a modern, all-inclusive complex, the development integrates comprehensive amenities, commercial services, and leisure facilities to cater to the diverse needs of residents in living, working, learning, and entertainment.

With a scale of nearly 3,000 units, The Gió Riverside promises to offer future homeowners an ideal riverside living experience, contributing to the creation of a "new breeze" - a fresh and modern lifestyle in Eastern Saigon.

Information

  • Alternative name: The Gió Riverside
  • Location: Eastern Saigon
  • Developer: An Gia Group

  • Expected Handover: 2027

Developer

An Gia Group (An Gia) is a reputable real estate developer specializing in the mid-end segment in Ho Chi Minh City and the southern market.

Founded in 2006, An Gia was privatized in 2012 and transitioned into a project developer in 2014. The company has made its mark with numerous prestigious awards and signature projects, including Westgate, The Standard, The Sóng, Sky89, River Panorama, Skyline, among others.

In 2020, An Gia was officially listed on the Ho Chi Minh City Stock Exchange under stock code AGG, marking a significant milestone in its journey as a public company.

For over a decade, An Gia has been dedicated to delivering mid-end housing with premium-quality standards. The company has successfully handed over 10 projects, supplying the market with more than 7,000 residential units.
